Spijkerplaat

Some Spikes and a Plate will result in a plate which will damage any mob walking over it, me and my peers don’t seem to take any damage though. The powers of the Dark Gem seems to ensure killing mobs in this process to drop the same things as when killed by any player.

For your convenience, they also give off a redstone signal when a mob is taking damage.
